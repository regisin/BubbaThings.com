**C﻿oop vs Run**

A﻿ coop is where they live and where they lay eggs.

A﻿ run is a secured area where they play and can run around. People who free range sometimes choose to have a run as well so that their yardbirds can be locked up away from predators if they go out of town. A lot of people also choose to free range during the day and lock their chickens up at night.

C﻿oops and Runs are often combined, and the chickens often have access to both areas 24/7 but will often naturally choose to go inside the coop when sleeping. \
\
**Automated Chicken Coop Door**

H﻿aving automated doors that allow your chickens in and out of the coop helps take a task out of your life. You can set the doors to close at dusk and to open at sunrise, you can get doors that lock so that predators can't open it from the outside, and you can get them solar powered. They come in a variety of colors. You can also DIY these.

**Roll Away Nest Boxes**

The longer an egg sits where the chicken laid it, the more likely that they end up covered with poop or will begin sitting on it trying to hatch it. I they have an opportunity to sit on their eggs, they will, and then they won't lay eggs. Roll away nest boxes are designed to catch the egg after it's hatched, and roll the egg away to a collection place away from the chickens, so that you can collect it.
